HOW TO PLAY

Objective

Be the last one standing as the storm closes in.

Controls

WASD move, Mouse aim, LMB fire, RMB aim/scope, R reload, Space jump/vault, C crouch/slide, Shift sprint, G grenade, 1-3 swap weapon, Tab inventory, V camera, F pick up, H medkit, Esc pause.

Loot

Walk over glowing crates and press F to grab weapons, ammo, armor and meds.

The Storm

The safe zone shrinks in phases. Outside it you take damage. Watch the timer.

Combat

Headshots deal more damage. Armor absorbs damage. Aim for the head.

Revive

There is no revive. Loot fast, fight smart.
